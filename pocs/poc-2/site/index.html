Our Story

We Meet... for the second time.

9th February 2017

We actually met 4 months prior to this day, on a drinks date at the Mile Castle Wetherspoons in Newcastle (classic!). For reasons we still agree to disagree over, there wasn't a second date.
Fast forward 4 months, a chance run-in whilst out in Newcastle, with their own friend groups, reignited conversations, and we've been inseperable since; maybe things do happen for a reason?

Our First Holiday

4th July 2017

Prior to 2017, Nathan hadn't been abroad in at least 10 years, but, after working extremely hard to get through their university exams that year, we felt we'd more than earned a trip away, and chose to go to Barcelona together. It was a fantastic first trip, filled with lots of tapas, walking, sightseeing, and cocktails. It gave Nathan the opportunity to see the Cam Nou, Barcelona's iconic football stadium, in person; ticking it off his football bucket list.

The Proposal

9th February 2023

Ask either of us how long overdue this was and you'll get very different answers! I knew I wanted to propose to Chloe back in 2021, after the *ahem*, challenges that year provided.
