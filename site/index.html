The Happy Couple

Nathan Stephenson

Nathan grew up in Gateshead with mum Shirley, dad John, and little brother Adam. Though reserved, he's always got time for everyone, whether it's helping out, tech support, or dabbling in a bit of DIY.

When not working as an IT consultant, he's usually gaming, running, or watching sports of some form, usually football and F1. Other than that, his favourite activity is time spent with Chloe, whether it's watching TV, shopping, or having a good ol' explore. Nathan's favourite things about Chloe are her honesty, silliness, and how she brings him out of his shell.

On the wedding day, you'll most likely find Nathan in the quieter areas, taking it all in and making sure everyone's having a good time!

Chloe Timlin

Chloe is a Corporate Events Manager at Lane 7, where she thrives on organizing memorable experiences and managing the intricate details of high-profile events.

She proudly claims the title of the "funny one" in her relationship with Nathan, always aiming to bring a smile to those around her with her quick wit and playful humor; but is deeply loyal and honest to her friends and family.

One of her favorite things about Nathan is his unwavering kindness and support, not to mention when he makes her laugh without even trying. Chloe loves nothing more watching TV with Nathan, whether they're diving into a gripping true crime series, laughing along with RuPaul's Drag Race, or cheering on their favorite F1 drivers.

On the wedding day, you'll find Chloe looking absolutely stunning, dancing the night away and chatting with as many of her extended family as possible!
